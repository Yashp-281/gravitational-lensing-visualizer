Physics grounding: event horizon size follows the real Schwarzschild radius relation (Rs ∝ mass); the photon sphere — where light can theoretically orbit the black hole — sits at exactly 1.5× Rs in real General Relativity, reproduced here as the default. The light-bending visual itself uses a simplified screen-space radial displacement (a common real-time approximation technique, not a full relativistic geodesic ray-tracer) — each background pixel is resampled outward from the black hole with a falloff proportional to 1/r, which qualitatively reproduces lensing arcs and Einstein-ring-like distortion without solving the full null-geodesic equations per photon. The accretion disk is a stylized illustrative element inspired by real lensed-disk imagery (e.g. the Event Horizon Telescope, and physically-informed renders like Interstellar's Gargantua) rather than a physically ray-traced disk.
